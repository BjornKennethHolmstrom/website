## Dedication

*For everyone who has achieved everything they were supposed to want,*  
*and still felt empty.*

*For those who work harder than ever,*  
*yet feel further from rest.*

*For the successful who are secretly suffering,*  
*the productive who are profoundly tired,*  
*and the high-achievers who have lost the ability to simply be.*

*This book is for you—not because you're broken,*  
*but because the systems we built are.*
