# The Averaging Problem Explorable

Demonstrates that a centralized controller seeing only a system mean cannot distinguish local crises from mild system‑wide fluctuations. The uniform response under‑responds where needed and disrupts healthy nodes.

## Dynamics

Based on the Governance Stability Simulator (Paper I). Ten coupled nodes, each with stability state. A shock hits nodes 2 and 7. The player can toggle between centralized control (mean observation, long latency, high noise) and distributed control (local observation, low latency, low noise).

## Key Learning

- In centralized mode, the dashboard looks acceptable while local crises deepen.
- Press "Reveal Node Details" after the simulation to see the hidden destruction.
- Switching to distributed control dramatically reduces collateral damage.
