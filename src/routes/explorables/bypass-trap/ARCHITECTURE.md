# The Bypass Trap Explorable

## What it demonstrates

A dysfunctional core institution spawns a bypass that works. As the bypass succeeds, public pressure to fix the core fades. Without a deliberate sunset condition, the core is never reformed—and may collapse entirely.

## Key mechanic

Toggle the **Sunset Condition** to see two trajectories:
- **OFF (the trap)**: bypass thrives, core decays, reform pressure vanishes.
- **ON (the catalyst)**: after a delay, sunset triggers renewed pressure, forcing core reform.

## Learning

Bypasses are rational responses to blocked institutions, but without explicit sunset conditions they become permanent alternatives rather than catalysts for core reform.
