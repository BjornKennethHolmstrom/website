# The Coordination Failure Tax Explorable

## What it demonstrates

Four sliders represent the capacity loss from four structural failure modes: Spatial Blindness, Frequency Mismatch, Preference Invisibility, and Observational Inadequacy. The page computes both the intuitive additive expectation and the actual multiplicative result.

## The insight

Governance failures do not add — they multiply. A system with four simultaneous failures at 50% capacity loss is not operating at zero capacity but at roughly 6% of baseline. That gap is the Coordination Failure Tax.
