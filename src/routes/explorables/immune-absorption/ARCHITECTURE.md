# The Immune Absorption Cycle Explorable

## What it simulates

A reform proposal travels through six institutional stages. At each stage, there is a probability of dilution — the reform gets watered down, celebrated publicly, but the underlying architecture remains unchanged. This is **symbolic adaptation**, the universal immune response identified in the Governance as Engineering series.

## Key mechanic

The player controls **immune permeability** — how effectively the institution blocks reform. At high permeability (default 0.7), structural reform is nearly impossible. At low permeability, reform passes through.

## Learning

- Most reforms don't fail — they get absorbed.
- The appearance of reform relieves pressure without structural change.
- The immune system is not an external barrier; it's the architecture's own defense.
