## 5. Working with the Grain: Transition Architecture for Bhutan

---

### 5.1 The Principle: Start Where Coupling Already Works

Start from local institutional leads and compare existing arrangements before considering imports or new bodies. Whether particular examples work remains an empirical question.

The original identifies environmental assessment (possible pre-commitment constraint), the tourism fee adjustment (possible adaptation), and the dated DHI ESG and Gelephu institutions (possible construction at pace). Evaluate actual legal application, decisions, ecological and social outcomes, review dates and alternatives independently. None establishes timely or effective constraint, successful adaptation or comparative superiority.

The original interprets the 2008 democratic transition, 2022 civil-service transformation and Gelephu initiative as evidence of considered royal initiative and continuity. These are historical leads, not a national-character generalization or inferred unanimity. Civil-service transformation and reopening borders are also potential causes of the resignation surge, independently of external comparison. Effective informal and formal arrangements, plural voices and independent challenge are genuine alternatives.

The original proposes a transparent, incremental transition based on validated local practice. Compare existing approaches, lawful responsibilities and independently assessed outcomes; do not infer consent from shared vocabulary or deference.

---

### 5.2 The Trojan Horse: GNH Materiality

The original uses “Trojan Horse” as a metaphor for carrying reform through a familiar instrument. It is retained as historical rhetoric only. The purpose, criteria, consequences and decision rights of any proposal must be explicit and open to affected people and independent challenge. No covert implementation or presumed approval.

The proposed instrument is **GNH materiality**: using a familiar ESG framework to consider specified national wellbeing commitments in commercial decisions, subject to lawful mandate and independent validation.

The original describes ESG as packaging familiar to the IFC, investors and development partners, and GNH as the payload. Its candidate selection is ecological diversity and resilience, community vitality, cultural diversity, good governance, time use and psychological wellbeing. It omits health, education and living standards. Materiality is a selection mechanism; disclose inclusion and exclusion reasons, task validity and uncertainty, distinguish financial materiality from impacts and national objectives, and test missing dimensions (W1/W4). Cultural criteria require prior plural interpretation and community-designated expertise; no exclusion or compensation of rights. Investor acceptance is unverified and not presumed.

The proposed framework might reach decisions that ordinary policy screening or conventional ESG handles differently. Compare actual scope, current ESG and no new layer, workload, delays, missed risks, inappropriate interventions and independent outcomes. Confidentiality must protect legitimate interests without preventing authorized independent challenge. No claim of unique efficacy, speed or automatic credibility.

---

### 5.3 Safe-to-Fail Pilots

The original proposes three pilots. They require lawful authorization, independent evaluation, affected-person participation, appropriate privacy protections, adequate resources and predefined stopping/rollback. Do not assume low cost, negligible risk, no delays or that limited duration contains harms.

**Pilot 1: GNH materiality at DHI.** The original proposes applying a GNH-anchored assessment to new investments above a defined size for two years, before commitment, with safe aggregate reporting. Hypothesis: specified assessment improves independently assessed decision appropriateness and outcomes relative to feasible alternatives within justified cost and delay. Compare existing ESG and no added layer, separate changes in funding and review where feasible (W7), predeclare cases, estimands, criteria, thresholds, uncertainty and protected rights. Count appropriate unchanged approvals, missed material risks, inappropriate modifications and actual outcomes, not just changed decisions. The original’s redesign rule was “If it adds delay without changing decisions, it should be redesigned.” This is an attributed starting proposal, not a criterion by itself (IC-o): unchanged decisions may be appropriate and review may provide benefits. Redesign or stop where independently assessed benefits do not justify burdens or risks. Independent review, recusal, appeals and safe stopping/redesign required. A justified nonadoption is legitimate.

**Pilot 2: a Gelephu baseline.** Retain the original regional GNH survey and ecological forest, river and wildlife-corridor baselines. The original calls this “the single most time-sensitive recommendation in this report” and argues that “A baseline taken now can never be reconstructed later”. That attributed urgency is not permission to rush. Establish actual development timing and historical data, co-design with affected residents and community-designated expertise, include displaced and excluded groups where safe and lawful; assess land acquisition, consent, compensation and ecological sampling. Voluntary accessible participation, refusal without disadvantage, minimization, separate sensitive data, no compulsory identifiers or tracking, safe storage and aggregate-only publication where safe. Repeated observations, comparison regions, nonresponse, spillovers and attrition need evaluation; before/after does not identify causal effects. Compare existing data/no new survey; stop if unsafe or unauthorized.

**Pilot 3: a decision-class register.** For twelve months the original proposes a record for Cabinet decisions above defined resource, duration and irreversibility thresholds, stating the review before commitment if any. The register could initially remain internal. Agency obligations require lawful authorization; voluntary individual participation is distinct. Include protected access, independent sampled records, emergency procedures, scope and rights, confidentiality, timeliness, error and workload; compare existing logs/no new requirement. The hypothesis concerns descriptive completeness and usefulness, not H0–H5. Stop or redesign if burden, error or harms outweigh benefits.

No pilot is authorized here. Their own hypotheses and comparators must be specified independently of diagnosis and political uptake. Safe stopping, privacy, review, and rollback or remediation of harms are required; appropriate approval, nonadoption and inconclusive outcomes are legitimate.

### 5.4 Upgrading the Slow Sensor

The original proposes extending GNH measurement to examine migration preferences, conditional on a legitimate task and valid measurement. Existing evidence and no new survey remain alternatives.

Optional survey questions on migration intentions, perceived career prospects and opportunity comparisons could help examine H2. They do not directly establish causal preferences or actual movement. Define estimand, validate questions with affected people, assess nonresponse and subgroup uncertainty, ensure voluntary, accessible and language-sensitive participation without disadvantage, nonidentifying by default; no employment, benefit or citizenship penalty, tracking or compelled disclosure.

The original also proposes an annual pulse on a subset of time use, psychological wellbeing and governance measures. Compare burden, reliability, selection, coverage and timely response against existing signals and no added collection. Do not assume distress, selection or faster cadence establishes need; no individual mental-health guidance or causal attribution. Data minimization, safe publication, protected refusal and lawful authorization apply.

---

### 5.5 Scaling by Attraction

The original proposes scaling by attraction: effective arrangements may generate interest. Adoption and investor recognition are political or market outcomes, not independent evidence of adequacy. Nonadoption and justified disagreement are legitimate.

DHI materiality and Gelephu reporting may enhance credibility for some investors, or add burdens without improving outcomes. Assess investor views, opportunity costs and independent ecological, social and economic outcomes. No inference of public benefit from capital attraction or adoption; compare rivals and consider conflicts of funders and reform providers.

The original tourism fee adjustment is a candidate precedent for public explanation and adaptation; verify confidence and actual outcomes separately, including adverse effects. Public rules and results may help others learn, but must not create ranking competition or compelled adoption. Protected confidentiality and valid disagreement remain.

---

### 5.6 Sequencing

The transition architecture does not need to wait for any one element to be complete before beginning the next, but the order of priority matters.

**First, make the landscape visible.** Consider the Actuator Interface Register against existing documentation and no new collection. Test accuracy, usefulness and burden; it does not confer new authority.

**Second, consider the timescale-matching rule.** Test whether structural revenue and buffer arrangements can support recurrent obligations under stress; compare feasible alternatives, costs and distribution. A published rule alone does not prevent recurrence.

**Third, test internal interfaces where risks warrant them.** Retain the DHI materiality and Gelephu baseline/reporting proposals, conditional on actual task risks, rights, evidence, costs and authorization, rather than assumed growth rate.

**Fourth, consider boundary frameworks.** The investment boundary and decision-class scope proposals could formalize tested arrangements if warranted by evidence, legal authority and affected-person participation. No automatic scaling sequence.

Two parallel tracks remain: career progression, portable certification and migration-cycle support on voluntary terms, and the independent population-boundary question. Assess rights, access, opportunities and wellbeing, not retention alone. Rights deliberation must not await economic reform or Register adoption.

### 5.7 The Role of the Centre: Make the Coupling Durable

The centre's most important contribution is to ensure that the interfaces outlast the moment that produced them.

The original asserts that the monarchy provides continuity of vision across elected governments and that “Gross National Happiness has survived every change of ruling party since 2008”. It interprets this as continuity of a long-term objective through political cycles. These historical and comparative claims are unverified; continuity as a mechanism for alignment is a hypothesis. Verify actual practice, competing perspectives and decision rights; neither continuity, formalization nor absence of dissent proves legitimacy or adequacy.

The task is to extend the same continuity to the interfaces themselves. Four measures would do so. **Legislate the frame, not the details**: place the decision-class triggers, the timescale-matching rule and the requirement for GNH materiality at state investors on a statutory basis, so that they cannot be quietly abandoned when pressure returns. **Strengthen independent review**: give the Royal Audit Authority, or a standing parliamentary committee, an explicit mandate to report on the interfaces around strategic investments and special jurisdictions, not only on their financial compliance. **Publish the rules**: make the frameworks governing DHI, the Royal Monetary Authority's strategic exposures and Gelephu's multidimensional commitments public, even where individual transactions remain confidential. **Protect the capacity**: treat the specialists who staff these interfaces as critical personnel in the civil service retention strategy, because interfaces without people are only paper.

These four measures are proposals, not enacted mandates. Statutory authority, legislative competence, independent funding and appointments, conflicts and recusal require review. The critical-personnel retention proposal creates possible differential pay, workload and staffing effects for nonselected personnel; measure equity, transfers and opportunity costs under W5/X2. No coerced retention, royal endorsement, consensus or immunity from challenge is inferred.

The original prefers distributed responsibility and existing oversight to a single new authority. Compare this option with effective existing arrangements and alternatives; confirm lawful ownership, resources and independent cross-institutional challenge without stealth mandate expansion.

The proposal seeks durable, contestable institutions capable of carrying legitimate aims. Whether this is preferable to existing arrangements remains open; transparent deliberation, rights and independent evidence are indispensable.
