## Appendix C: The Governance as Engineering Connection

### C.1 The Architectural Foundation

The Governance as Engineering series provides feedback-control analogies. They require specified tasks, disturbances, state variables, measurement and response assumptions; conditional Ashby is retained, not a proof of national adequacy. R4–R8 corrections and the provisional methodological synthesis are conceptual aids, not empirical evidence.

Bhutan was not among the Paper VIII pilot countries and has no Variety Gap estimate here. The accepted Korea correction withdraws its former estimate, interval, band, trend and unverified pilot-validation premises. No national score, trajectory or comparison is inferred.

### C.2 The Papers in Brief

**Paper II — Fractality as Stability.** The earlier single-rate impossibility inference and I–V bridge are withdrawn here. A fast controller can suffice in a stipulated model; distinguish sensing, processing, review, authorization and response clocks (W3).

**Paper VI — The Variety Gap.** The earlier objective-as-observation inference is withdrawn. Objective breadth, evidence quality and task information differ; dimensions named or excluded from objectives do not decide actual observability (W1).

**Paper XI — Reform Exhaustion.** Delegation-chain fidelity is a conceptual lead, not a verified law about Bhutan. The capstone retention inequality is conditional on its stipulated sequence; interfaces can interact positively or negatively (W8).

**Paper XIII — Legitimacy.** The original treats royal legitimacy as an integrative gain analogy. It is not an accepted quantified result or evidence of national consensus or effective review. Actual legitimacy, plural perspectives, independent challenge and decision rights require investigation.

### C.3 Observability, Controllability and the Interface Race

In specified control models, observability concerns reconstructibility and controllability reachability; no institutional analogy inherits these properties. The interface hypothesis concerns evidence, task ownership, feasible authorized responses, timing and implementation. Paper XX’s action-compatible cover is necessary only under its premises; it is not a sufficient adequacy test (W1/W2).

The original summarizes the proposed dynamic as the following diagnostic shorthand, not a comparable numerical relationship:

$$
\frac{dA}{dt} \quad \text{versus} \quad \frac{dI}{dt}
$$

Capital, autonomy, speed, irreversibility, coverage, review capacity, resources and deadlines are heterogeneous. Changing units can reverse apparent rate comparisons (W3). Do not infer an inequality without defined units, normalization, tasks and loss, or use such a comparison as a national criterion. Correction latency may matter, but no ranking of variables is established.

### C.4 What Bhutan Adds to the Framework

The original proposes a future model of an effective objective as an intersection of objectives and institutional interfaces. This is a conceptual lead, not a demonstrated quantity, theorem or national trajectory. Legitimate distributed tasks, protective constraints, conflicting objectives and effective existing arrangements must remain distinguishable. No national Korea objective-collapse inference survives. The organizational synthesis is provisional.

### C.5 Accepted conditional witnesses

All numerical values below are stipulated counterexamples, not measurements of Bhutan. Retain Ashby's law only conditionally: achieving a task-defined acceptable set under specified disturbances requires sufficiently distinguishable and feasible responses. A sensor, a value, a statutory mandate and a response are not interchangeable. Formal observability/controllability have model-specific definitions (e.g. reconstructibility/reachability in specified dynamical systems); no institutional analogue inherits those properties by naming them.

#### W1 — Objective coverage versus observation
For static linear noiseless states d in subspace D, response can ignore L⊆D if task-equivalent states differ by L. K=D∩ker C. With unrestricted compensating responses in D modulo L, K⊆L is the task-information condition; h=dim K−dim(K∩L). Lower bound max(0,dim D−dim L−rank(C|D)) is not sufficient. D=R³, L=span(e2,e3), C observes x1: h=0; C observes x2,x3: h=1 despite higher rank. Nine domains or 33 indicators do not determine C, D or L. An objective can name a dimension without measuring it, and a measured dimension can be outside the objective. (VI.)

#### W2 — Authority, action and implementation
For a task and information class E, a common feasible authorized action in intersection of acceptable action sets over E is necessary for a fixed response; XX action-compatible cover necessary under stated premises, not generally sufficient. Let d∈{0,1}, y=d, required a=d. If institution may only choose a=0 then d=1 fails despite exact observation. If another authorized owner can perform a=1, distributed arrangement may suffice; if execution fails, same observation and permission do not suffice. Conversely a task requiring only a=0 has no gap from this restriction. No mandate expansion inferred.

#### W3 — Sampling, timing and non-comparable rates
A single fast controller with exact current state and instantaneous unrestricted compensation can handle slow and fast disturbances in a stipulated static model; different rates alone do not establish impossibility. For binary state initially 0, flips at periods 3 and 7, reliable notification delayed two periods yields stale states at 3,4,7,8 out of periods 0–9. Event reporting with zero delay and known initial state is sufficient in this example; in practice reporting is endogenous and missing reports do not mean no issue. Separate sample, assessment, authorization, commitment, correction and recovery clocks. A=(capital,speed), I=(coverage,review capacity) have distinct units: even a scalar inequality can reverse under unit changes: rates 2 and 1 become 2 and 100 if I is expressed in units one hundred times smaller. No comparison dA/dt>dI/dt without declared operational normalization, task and loss. A missed hard deadline or irreversible harm is not retroactively prevented by later disclosure; later disclosure and remedy may still have value.

#### W4 — Sufficiency and proxies
Two stipulated profiles X=(0.8,0.4), Y=(0.6,0.9). Equal weights give .60 vs .75; weights (.8,.2) give .72 vs .66. Rankings depend on normative weights. With threshold .5 per component, X fails second and Y meets both; a score cannot waive a hard right. Within a binary threshold indicator, .6 and .9 both meet .5, but incentives to change other indicators, measurement, boundary or threshold persist. Neither universal tournament resistance nor actual wellbeing follows. Paper VIII: no validated index of adequacy from these components. XX proxy example: with proxy M=x, task T=x+y, lottery (2,0) with probability 1/4 else (0,0) gives E M=E T=1/2; deterministic (0,1) gives M=0,T=1. Higher proxy not higher task performance.

#### W5 — Fiscal time mismatch and interface burden
Stipulated real recurrent obligation 6 per year, structural revenue available 2 per year, extraordinary dividends 4 in years 1 and 2, zero thereafter. Annual gap 0,0,4; two years of finance do not establish sustainability. A buffer of 8 with no return and no new funding covers two additional gaps of 4 then is exhausted. Durable structural revenue, lawful reallocation, sustainable buffer rule or changed commitments could address the gap; volatile income alone does not prove insolvency or bad investment. The stipulated 6/4 mirrors the source’s Nu 6 bn/Nu 4 bn figures; 2 is a constructed structural-revenue value, not a Bhutan figure. No Bhutan forecast. For an assessment service with 100 time units, 40 essential other work, 20 administration, removing 10 administrative units while adding 6 reporting and 7 review units leaves 37 rather than 40 for task work. Independent review may still add value; count burden shifted to agencies, communities and firms, missed deadlines and prevented harms.

#### W6 — Population frames, migration and selection
Among 100 eligible persons, 20 respond, 12 report intent to migrate: respondent share 60%; without missingness assumptions population share bounded [12%,92%]. Nonresidents, expelled persons, noncitizens and people not in the frame are not covered by these bounds; the relevant frame is a legitimate question, not a correction by adding everyone to a resident survey. Aggregate GNH group mean .8 is compatible with migrants being concentrated in either high or low wellbeing subgroups. Example: two equal-sized baseline resident groups with means .6 and 1.0 have aggregate mean .8. If the migrant cohort is no larger than either subgroup, that observed baseline mean is compatible with all migrants coming from the .6 subgroup or all from the 1.0 subgroup; the aggregate does not identify migrant selection. Distinguish stated intent, unique movers, trips, duration, returns and involuntary displacement; refusal is legitimate. No linkage without lawful authority and privacy safeguards.

#### W7 — Causal identification and decision changes
For (status quo, review only, funding only, both), outcome tables (0,2,0,2) and (0,0,2,2) give the same bundle improvement 2 but review effects 2 and 0. Even randomized bundle cannot identify components. Review-induced investment changes, or one visible costly veto, do not establish net benefit; correct approvals and existing controls may be better. Likewise attrition decline after pay rise does not identify wage effect apart from border, reform, labor demand, cohort and other changes. Predeclare estimands, comparators, harms, timing, spillovers, uncertainty; do not force a veto for evaluation.

#### W8 — Delegation, interactions and adaptation
For a stated sequential retention model with losses ell_i∈[0,1], retained fraction Π(1−ell_i) ≥ max(0,1−Σell_i), not an empirical law for a hierarchy or legitimacy. With outcome values baseline 10, A 12, B 13, AB 14, interaction −1; AB 17 yields +2. Interfaces can duplicate, conflict, complement or repair earlier deficiencies. Compare–Exit–Accelerate–Recouple is not a closed-loop theorem; a link can fail, adaptation can break it. XI and capstone conditional only; XIII gain analogy no quantitative inference.

#### W9 — Review plurality and common-mode error
For two equal marginal hazard miss probabilities p and correlation rho, joint miss J=p²+rho p(1−p), subject to Frechet bounds. p=1/20 yields independent J=1/400; J≤1/100 requires rho≤3/19, rho_min=−1/19. Both-must-flag miss=2p−J (39/400 under independence). Reviewer agreement is not hazard detection, false alarm control or timely authorized correction. No assumption that RAA, Cabinet, ESG panels or AI reviewers are independent merely because named separately. Subsidiarity agreement-event distinction. Apply to this audit itself.
