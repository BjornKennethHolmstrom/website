## 5. A Concrete First Step: The Monetary Policy Distributional Impact Assessment

### 5.1 The Logic of the First Step

The original selects an independent distributional Assessment as a near-term intervention, accompanied by a Model Humility Red Team and a Citizens' Assembly Pilot. The rationale is to examine potentially important limitations in the existing decision pathway through a more bounded experiment than comprehensive mandate and instrument reform. These are specific candidate interventions, not the demonstrated most catalytic first step. Their feasibility, marginal benefit and institutional owner must be established in the relevant jurisdiction. If the bottleneck is authority or implementation rather than evidence, additional sensing or publication cannot by itself address it. If the existing arrangements are adequate, no additional reporting may be the appropriate decision.

The Assessment investigates the distributional channels through which policy may affect different groups. It does not establish that those channels are currently unseen. A policy committee may already have evidence but face legitimate mandate constraints, competing objectives or a response better allocated to another authority. The Red Team investigates consequential model limitations and the ability to correct them. The Assembly explores public understanding and reasoned deliberation on trade-offs. Each proposal has its own evidence requirements and independent outcomes; no experiment requires a commitment to the full architecture. The research questions and possible policy implications should be disclosed to participants and decision-makers, not framed tactically to evade scrutiny.

### 5.2 The Monetary Policy Distributional Impact Assessment

The proposed Assessment is a regularly prepared, independently conducted scenario analysis of the distributional consequences of major policy decisions. The original design envisages a dedicated Distributional Analysis Unit, protected independence and secure funding, publication alongside decisions and a mandatory written response by the relevant committee. A statutory body or mandatory response may need legislative authorization even without a change in the bank's monetary objectives. Compare a voluntary pilot, independent statistical or research body, parliamentary analytical office and existing internal practice before adopting a statutory design. Preserve the committee's lawful discretion.

The original's action classes are retained:

- **Interest-rate decisions:** estimate distributional consequences through asset valuations, debt-service and deposit cash flows, employment and wages, inflation, housing and differential regional exposure to interest-sensitive activity. Disaggregate by income decile, wealth quintile, region and demographic categories such as age, housing tenure and employment status when justified and reliable. Distinguish groups' assets and liabilities and their indirect exposure to policy.
- **Asset purchase programmes:** assess government, corporate and mortgage-backed securities where relevant, distinguishing transaction structures, direct and indirect beneficiaries, sectoral and regional allocation, valuation effects, reserve remuneration and associated public risk. A capital gain is not by itself a net resource transfer; estimate incidence relative to a stated alternative and include fiscal effects and employment.
- **Forward guidance and communications:** compare the implications of signalled policy paths for borrowers, savers, equity holders, households without financial assets, investment and consumption. Establish the intended expectations mechanism and assess the effects of the Assessment's own publication on communication. Anticipation is not necessarily policy failure.

The Assessment presents scenarios, assumptions and sensitivity rather than a precise forecast of who will gain. Document the counterfactual, uncertainty from parameters and models, coverage limitations and the distributional metric. Publish uncertainty ranges when supportable and qualitative limitations when not. No specific bottom-50/top-10 transfer is asserted without causal evidence. The same evidence is used to evaluate favourable and unfavourable incidence. A short-horizon change in valuations may differ from longer-term effects through work, prices, fiscal policy and financial stability. Independent methodological challenge should include dispersed sources of knowledge and alternative institutions capable of addressing the task.

The original proposes a **Distributional Analysis Unit** with a fixed, non-renewable director appointed through a cross-partisan parliamentary committee, multi-stakeholder panel or independent statistical authority. These are alternative appointment designs, not existing legal arrangements. Compare conflicts, competence, continuity and accountability; protect funding and analytical judgments while allowing methodological scrutiny and correction. No policy committee clearance should suppress findings simply because it dislikes them; legitimate confidentiality and security controls need transparent procedures and independent oversight. Lawful access to necessary household balance-sheet information, regional indicators, distributional accounts or securities holdings requires purpose limitation, data minimisation, aggregation and other less intrusive alternatives. Do not claim statutory access to all data is necessary. Record access, retention, independent audits, reidentification risk, group harms and remedy.

**Timing is a design variable.** Define the preparation cutoff, secure predecision delivery to authorised committee members, decision time and public release separately. If a scenario analysis is intended to inform a decision, it must reach the relevant decision-makers early enough for consideration. It may be released alongside the decision, or delayed for a documented lawful market-sensitivity reason, subject to fair disclosure and review. An arrangement where the committee first receives the Assessment simultaneously with the public after deciding is an ex-post accountability mechanism; it cannot inform the completed decision. Evaluate subsequent effects separately. Predecision access requires safeguards against leakage, unfair trading advantages, interference and inappropriate reliance. The publication itself may signal a future policy path, so test communication and market expectations rather than presume publication has no policy effect.

The **mandatory response obligation** is a candidate reason-giving mechanism. Where lawfully required, the committee responds publicly in writing within a specified period, explaining the relevant evidence, its judgment about trade-offs under the mandate, disagreement with the methodology, and any matters referred to another competent body. The original illustrates this by asking how a committee might explain a decision that a scenario predicts will harm low-income households. Such an illustration is not a finding about the sign of rate changes. The duty is to give reasons, not to agree, redistribute or change rates. Evaluate substantive engagement and the independent quality and effects of decisions; a response count does not establish accountability. A requirement that exceeds lawful authority must be redesigned or declined.

### 5.3 Complementary Mechanisms: The Model Humility Red Team and the Citizens' Assembly Pilot

**The Model Humility Red Team** is the original's proposed permanent challenge function, protected from modelling staff and sufficiently resourced to test assumptions, produce alternative scenarios and identify consequential limitations. It would present its work alongside baseline forecasts at meetings, and publish an annual Model Limitations Report documenting omitted distinctions, failed predictions and relevant scenarios, subject to lawful confidentiality. The proposal includes independent appointments and funding, protected escalation, reasoned response and recorded follow-up, and oversight of the unit itself. Compare independent external forecast evaluation and existing internal challenge. Common models and data may create correlated misses; false alarms and resource costs also matter. Assess whether criticisms are reliable, consequential and acted on where appropriate, not simply whether the unit disagrees more often. Career incentives should reward sound correction, not obligatory contrarianism.

**The Citizens' Assembly Pilot on Monetary Policy Trade-offs** is a randomly selected, demographically inclusive body convened for a defined period with professional facilitation, balanced expert testimony and public, reasoned nonbinding recommendations. The original names a central bank, parliamentary committee or independent foundation as possible initiators. Define the authority of the sponsor, fair funding, accessibility, compensation, agenda and methods, disclose conflicts, record disagreement and explain sampling limits. Possible questions include price stability and employment, asset purchases, moral hazard and climate-related risks. Participants do not set rates or authorize a new mandate. Existing hearings, consultations and independent public deliberation are comparators. Test comprehension, deliberative quality, participation costs, inclusive access and accountable responses; disagreement with the report is not failure. No community or public endorsement is inferred from a pilot's design.

### 5.4 Selection Criteria: Why These?

The original proposes three reasons for selecting these mechanisms. First, each addresses a different possible bottleneck: distributional information or accountability, model challenge and public deliberation. Whether these bottlenecks actually exist for a task must be established; existing arrangements or a different owner may perform the function better. Second, the proposals can be explored in bounded ways without committing to a comprehensive redesign. The original characterises them as small, reversible probes. In practice, a statutory independent unit, protected access or mandatory response may require new law, funding and durable obligations; even a temporary assembly or internal red team requires resources, authority and safeguards. Design exit, review and stop conditions before enrolment or data collection.

Third, the original anticipates that public analysis, model challenge and deliberation could generate further learning, public questions and precedents for institutional design. This is a learning rationale, not a strategy for creating political demand irrespective of evidence. Legislators may ask questions about a distributional scenario; modelling staff may revise assumptions; a pilot may clarify trade-offs. They may also find existing arrangements sufficient, reject particular proposals or identify another priority. No specific wealth-transfer estimate, causal mechanism or public constituency is assumed. Compare the information generated and decisions improved with less costly and less centralising alternatives. The aims and possible subsequent options must be transparent; no avoidance of scrutiny is part of the design.

### 5.5 How to Measure Success

Preregister intervention-specific endpoints, comparison arrangements, the evaluation horizon, plausible confounders and the method of identification before observing results. Separate the diagnosis tests in §1.7 from evaluating these proposals. The adoption or rejection of an architecture, and a null or adverse result in its evaluation, do not by themselves test H0–H5. A study may inform a diagnostic question only through appropriate independent evidence of the proposed mechanism. Use matched episodes or credible comparative designs where feasible; document selection, attrition, lags and common-model uncertainty. Independent evaluation should not depend exclusively on the body that designed the intervention.

For the **Assessment**, establishment of the unit, funding, timely publication, responses, media attention, parliamentary discussion and adoption by other institutions are process indicators. Evaluate incremental evidence and forecast quality, calibration, accessibility, comprehension, reason quality, whether predecision information affects appropriate deliberation, and effects on independent outcomes. Compare costs, inflation and expectation effects, financial stability, distributional outcomes, privacy, leakage, information fairness and rights. A decision unchanged for sound reasons may be appropriate; a policy change may be harmful. Compare existing research and no extra reporting. Postdecision reporting and predecision influence have distinct measures.

For the **Red Team**, a Model Limitations Report, scenario presentation and staff responses indicate implementation. Evaluate the relevance and accuracy of challenge, independent detection of material errors, forecast performance, correction and decision quality, false alarms and costs. Account for correlated errors, shared data, and whether alternative perspectives are genuinely able to contest assumptions. Publication does not itself establish humility. A reasoned refusal of an irrelevant criticism can be a good result.

For the **Assembly Pilot**, completion, satisfaction, public recommendations, institutional response and diffusion are process or experience indicators. Evaluate informed understanding, quality and diversity of reasons, inclusion, independence, representativeness limits, fair participation and whether accountable decisions are supported. Distinguish those outcomes from agreement or institutionalisation. A recommendation not adopted for lawful and reasoned grounds is not proof of procedural absorption. No participation metric substitutes for public authorization.

The ultimate evaluation is not whether the first step enables the second. It is whether each intervention delivers justified incremental benefits for its specified purpose, net of burdens and risks, compared with the relevant alternatives. Further research, targeted modification, no change, legitimate nonadoption or stopping may be appropriate outcomes. Null and adverse results are valuable for intervention learning. A reform that simply generates further demand for itself has not demonstrated public value.

### 5.6 The Honest Acknowledgment

The original identifies possible failure modes worth investigating: an Assessment established but selectively cited or ignored; a Red Team marginalised through budget or staffing changes; an Assembly whose recommendations and lessons receive no meaningful consideration. These could indicate genuine procedural absorption if the relevant challenge was sound, within scope and actionable, and the response was unjustifiably obstructed. H5 provides observational coding of reasons, authority, actual changes and correction. Another possibility is legitimate nonadoption, or a finding that the proposed design has weak analytical value, disproportionate costs or no lawful basis. Both must be allowed. The future institutional response cannot be inferred from a metaphor or from professional identity.

The original argues that building an informational and experimental apparatus may be worth attempting in the face of uncertainty. That is the proposal's motivation, not a prediction that the alternative must be worsening crisis or delegitimation. A pilot should be stopped or redesigned if lawful data access, independence, market fairness, participation or proportionate benefit cannot be established. Preserve the capacity to learn from failure without treating every objection as a symptom of the diagnosis. The engineers are at the table; the question is what evidence and responsibilities will support effective and accountable correction.
