## Appendix C: Bibliography and Source Notes

### C.1 On French Governance and the Jacobin Tradition

- Rosanvallon, P. (2008). *Counter-Democracy: Politics in an Age of Distrust*. Cambridge University Press. Foundational analysis of the rise of mistrust and the forms of counter-democratic oversight in contemporary democracies, with deep relevance to France.
- Rosanvallon, P. (2011). *Democratic Legitimacy: Impartiality, Reflexivity, Proximity*. Princeton University Press. Explores new forms of legitimacy needed when electoral legitimacy erodes.
- Hayward, J. (2007). *Fragmented France: Two Centuries of Disputed Identity*. Oxford University Press. Traces the long history of French political fragmentation and the recurring tension between centralisation and contestation.
- Duran, P. (2010). *Penser l’action publique*. LGDJ. A sociological analysis of the gap between policy conception and implementation in the French state.
- Cole, A. (2008). *Governing and Governance in France*. Cambridge University Press. Comprehensive overview of the French governance architecture and its evolution.

### C.2 On Territorial Reform and the Integration Deficit

- Le Galès, P. (2002). *European Cities: Social Conflicts and Governance*. Oxford University Press. Though European in scope, provides analytical tools for understanding territorial governance gaps.
- Pasquier, R., Simoulin, V., & Weisbein, J. (2013). *La gouvernance territoriale*. LGDJ. Synthesis of territorial governance practices in France.
- Epstein, R. (2013). *La rénovation urbaine: Démolition-reconstruction de l’État*. Presses de Sciences Po. Demonstrates how national urban policy is implemented—and often distorted—at the local level.

### C.3 On Trust, Deliberation, and the Inner Deficit

- Giraudet, L.‑G., et al. (2021). “Deliberating on Climate Action: Insights from the French Citizens’ Convention for Climate.” *Ecological Economics*. Detailed analysis of the Convention’s process, outputs, and political reception.
- Bourg, D., & Whiteside, K. (2010). *Vers une démocratie écologique: Le citoyen, le savant et le politique*. Seuil. Argues for embedding ecological deliberation into democratic institutions.
- Blondiaux, L. (2008). *Le nouvel esprit de la démocratie: Actualité de la démocratie participative*. Seuil. A leading French scholar on participatory democracy, its promises and limits.
- Mansbridge, J., et al. (2012). “A Systemic Approach to Deliberative Democracy.” In Parkinson, J., & Mansbridge, J. (eds.), *Deliberative Systems*. Cambridge University Press. Provides the theoretical framework for thinking about deliberation as a system rather than isolated events.

### C.4 On the Gilets Jaunes and Territorial Abandonment

- Collectif d’enquête sur les Gilets Jaunes (2019). *Gilets Jaunes: Enquête sur une révolte*. Seuil. Sociological investigation of the movement’s roots in territorial and social fractures.
- Fourquet, J. (2019). *L’archipel français: Naissance d’une nation multiple et divisée*. Seuil. Maps the cultural and territorial fragmentation underlying contemporary French politics.

### C.5 On Spiral Dynamics and Value Systems

- Beck, D. E., & Cowan, C. C. (1996). *Spiral Dynamics: Mastering Values, Leadership, and Change*. Blackwell. The foundational text.
- Wilber, K. (2000). *A Theory of Everything: An Integral Vision for Business, Politics, Science, and Spirituality*. Shambhala. Places the framework within broader integral theory.
- Laloux, F. (2014). *Reinventing Organizations*. Nelson Parker. Documents organisations operating from integrative consciousness.

### C.6 On Policy Experimentation and Adaptive Governance

- Ansell, C., & Gash, A. (2008). “Collaborative Governance in Theory and Practice.” *Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory*, 18(4), 543–571.
- Duit, A., & Galaz, V. (2008). “Governance and Complexity.” *Governance*, 21(3), 311–335.
- Folke, C., et al. (2005). “Adaptive Governance of Social-Ecological Systems.” *Annual Review of Environment and Resources*, 30, 441–473.
