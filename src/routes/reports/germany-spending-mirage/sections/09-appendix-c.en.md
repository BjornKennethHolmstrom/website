## Appendix C: Bibliography and Source Notes

### C.1 On Adaptive Capacity and Governance

- Duit, A., & Galaz, V. (2008). "Governance and Complexity—Emerging Issues for Governance Theory." *Governance*, 21(3), 311–335. Foundational text on what complex systems theory implies for governance design.
- Folke, C., et al. (2005). "Adaptive Governance of Social-Ecological Systems." *Annual Review of Environment and Resources*, 30, 441–473. Establishes the concept of adaptive governance and its application to resource management.
- Ansell, C., & Gash, A. (2008). "Collaborative Governance in Theory and Practice." *Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory*, 18(4), 543–571. Comprehensive review of conditions for successful cross-sector collaboration.

### C.2 On Inner Capacity and Cognitive Infrastructure

- Kegan, R., & Lahey, L. L. (2009). *Immunity to Change: How to Overcome It and Unlock the Potential in Yourself and Your Organization*. Harvard Business Press. Grounds the "immune system" metaphor in adult developmental psychology.
- Miller, R. (Ed.) (2018). *Transforming the Future: Anticipation in the 21st Century*. UNESCO/Routledge. Comprehensive treatment of futures literacy as a capability.
- Boyatzis, R. E., & McKee, A. (2005). *Resonant Leadership: Renewing Yourself and Connecting with Others Through Mindfulness, Hope, and Compassion*. Harvard Business School Press. Connects emotional and cognitive capacity to decision-making quality and leadership effectiveness.

### C.3 On German Governance and Reform Challenges

- Mair, P., & Thomassen, J. (2010). "Political Representation and Government in Representative Democracies." *European Political Science Review*, 2(1), 1–24. Contextualises the challenges of responsiveness and responsibility in modern governance.
- Scharpf, F. W. (1988). "The Joint-Decision Trap: Lessons from German Federalism and European Integration." *Public Administration*, 66(3), 239–278. Classic diagnosis of the coordination problems inherent in German federalism.
- Czada, R. (2021). "Germany's Energy Transition: A Case Study in Incremental Radicalism." In *The Routledge Handbook of Policy Styles*. Traces the successes and limitations of Germany's Energiewende governance.

### C.4 On Spiral Dynamics and Value Systems

- Beck, D. E., & Cowan, C. C. (1996). *Spiral Dynamics: Mastering Values, Leadership, and Change*. Blackwell. The foundational text; introduces the full value systems framework.
- Wilber, K. (2000). *A Theory of Everything: An Integral Vision for Business, Politics, Science, and Spirituality*. Shambhala. Places Spiral Dynamics within the broader integral theory of development.
- Laloux, F. (2014). *Reinventing Organizations*. Nelson Parker. Documents organisations operating from integrative consciousness, with practical lessons for institutional design.

### C.5 On Digital Infrastructure and Policy Innovation

- Mergel, I., Edelmann, N., & Haug, N. (2019). "Defining Digital Transformation: Results from Expert Interviews." *Government Information Quarterly*, 36(4). Maps the concept of digital transformation in the public sector.
- O'Reilly, T. (2017). *WTF? What's the Future and Why It's Up to Us*. Harper Business. Articulates principles for building platforms that enable rather than control.
- Kattel, R., Lember, V., & Tõnurist, P. (2020). "Collaborative Innovation and Human-Machine Networks." *Public Management Review*, 22(7). Explores how AI and digital platforms reshape public sector innovation capacity.
