## Appendix E: About this essay and how it was made

### How it was made

This essay was generated by AI language models (Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek) from prompts by Björn Kenneth Holmström. He chose the topic, posed the questions and decided to publish the result, but wrote little of the text himself.

The essay has not been systematically fact-checked. Statements about German institutions, laws, budgets, events, figures and sources may be inaccurate or invented, and should be verified against primary sources before being relied on or cited.

### Perspective and limits

Neither the models nor the person who prompted them write from within the policy establishment. Björn Kenneth Holmström has studied engineering physics and has a long-standing personal interest in systems thinking, complexity and governance. He has no formal training or professional experience in German public administration, economics or political science. The framework applied here draws on complexity science, developmental psychology and governance theory.

That distance limits access to the granular, day-to-day texture of policy-making. An outside lens can sometimes raise questions that the centre has learned not to hear, but it can just as easily miss what insiders know. The essay does not claim insider knowledge. It offers a lens that may or may not prove useful to those who hold institutional positions and are searching for frameworks that make sense of what they are experiencing.

Feedback, criticism, and corrections are welcome. The argument is offered in the spirit of collaborative sense-making, not definitive pronouncement.
