## Appendix F: Editorial verification and open questions (12 November 2026)

This appendix documents a **targeted source check**, not a comprehensive verification or endorsement of the report’s conclusions. The report’s Competence Trap, Drift Loop, Data Archipelago, and Municipal Trap remain conceptual hypotheses. Claims of country-wide causation or comparative superiority require dedicated empirical study. The independent companion analysis proposed for later work is **not** part of this revision.

### F.1 Primary and institutional sources consulted

1. **Inter-agency sharing:** [Government of Sweden, 1 December 2025](https://www.regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2025/12/nu-rivs-sekretessen-mellan-alla-myndigheter-i-sverige/); [Government Bill 2024/25:180](https://www.regeringen.se/rattsliga-dokument/proposition/2025/05/prop.-202425180); [Skolverket guidance on sharing among schools, social services and police](https://www.skolverket.se/larande-och-trygghet/trygghet-vardegrund-och-arbetsmiljo/brottsforebyggande-arbete/dela-information-mellan-skola-socialtjanst-och-polis). The reform creates conditional opportunities; it does not authorise arbitrary linkage or remove all secrecy.
2. **NATO chronology and earlier cooperation:** [Government of Sweden, historical relations with NATO](https://www.government.se/government-policy/sweden-in-nato/history-of-sweden-and-nato). The rapid 2022 decision is evidence of a policy pivot, not sufficient evidence of suppressed warnings.
3. **Municipal equalisation:** [SKR, the equalisation system](https://skr.se/ekonomiochbudgetplanering/utjamningssystem.8275.html). The system is designed to offset differences in tax capacity and structural needs; specific residual mismatches must be demonstrated.
4. **Municipal trials:** [Government assignment to Statskontoret, 14 May 2025](https://www.regeringen.se/regeringsuppdrag/2025/05/uppdrag-till-statskontoret-att-analysera-och-foresla-regelforenklingar-och-forsoksverksamheter-for-okad-effektivitet-i-kommunal-verksamhet/). The proposed Framtidskommuner Act is an additional policy proposal, not existing law.
5. **Sámi consultation and rights context:** [Government Bill 2021/22:19](https://www.regeringen.se/rattsliga-dokument/proposition/2021/10/prop.-20212219); [Sametinget, mining in Sápmi (2026)](https://sametinget.se/1811). Consultation duties and rights issues are distinct from ordinary municipal coordination. This revision does not pronounce on the legality of any specific proposed project.
6. **Data-protection impact assessments:** [Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY)](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/konsekvensbedomning/nar-ska-en-konsekvensbedomning-genomforas/). Technical privacy measures do not replace a lawful basis or proportionality assessment.

### F.2 Claims still requiring independent investigation

- Whether a distinctive Swedish Drift Loop can be identified in multiple domains, rather than inferred from selected examples.
- Whether *Jantelagen* and the proposed Mean-Reversion Fallacy explain measurable institutional behavior; these are metaphors until operationalised and tested.
- The comparative ranking of Swedish digital-government fragmentation and the causal contribution of each legal, technical, and organizational barrier.
- The size and distribution of municipal capacity constraints **after** tax and cost equalisation, and how constraints differ across policy fields.
- Comparisons with Germany and France, which are interpretative shorthand rather than validated country diagnoses.
- Costing, legal feasibility, baseline outcome measures, selection design, and possible adverse effects of the Framtidskommuner pilots.

### F.3 Revision principle

Correct demonstrable errors in the original report; retain its central argument while making its evidentiary limits explicit. Reserve more extensive inquiries into power, distribution, intersecting disadvantage, and decolonial critique for the separately scoped companion analysis, then revise the main architecture if that analysis warrants it.
