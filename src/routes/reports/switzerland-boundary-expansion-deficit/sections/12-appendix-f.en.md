## Appendix F: Glossary

Terms are diagnostic or historical vocabulary, not validated measurements. Swiss institutional descriptions require source verification. See Appendix C for model scope.

**Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety** (GaE) — A model-dependent relation between disturbances, available responses and tolerated outcomes; not a universal dimension-count or geographic criterion.

**Asymmetric Observation** (GaE) — Hypothesis of task-relevant private information insufficiently connected to public decisions; distinguish absence, access, use and authority.

**Bilaterals I / II / III** (CH) — Packages of Swiss–EU agreements; see dated account in Chapter 3.

**Binnenland** (GaE) — The domestic institutional domain in the report; not an assessment of its performance.

**Boundary Expansion Deficit** (GaE) — Hypothesised binding mismatch between a task’s consequence footprint and mandates or coordination; not a scalar or an automatic reason to expand.

**Bypass Trap** (GaE) — Hypothesis that local alternatives relieve pressure for further change; complement, integration and no further change are also possible.

**Faraday Cage** (GaE) — Historical metaphor for potential differences in information access under neutrality, not a physical or general causal law.

**Frequency Mismatch** (GaE) — Possible mismatch between response timing and task deadlines or disturbance dynamics; specify the pathway and acceptable response.

**Goodhart-Ashby Synthesis** (GaE) — Historical synthesis revised by XX: proxy ordering depends on the feasible set, ties and domain. Omitted objectives do not imply lost information.

**Governance Problem Hierarchy** (GaE) — Historical grouping of observation, response, integration and boundary questions; no necessary sequence or stage ranking.

**Guillotine Clause** (CH) — Linkage among Bilaterals I agreements; actual scope and termination consequences require legal verification.

**Immune System** (GaE) — Metaphor for candidate resistance mechanisms; distinguish vested interests, legitimate safeguards and ordinary disagreement.

**Konkordanz** (CH) — Consensus and power-sharing tradition; the operation of actual rules requires episode-specific analysis.

**Masseneinwanderungsinitiative** (CH) — 2014 initiative discussed in Chapter 3; verify text and implementation.

**Observation Channel** (GaE) — Pathway carrying task-relevant information. Its usefulness depends on content, reliability, timing, access and task; distinct from evaluation and authority.

**Reactive Fudge Loop** (GaE) — Candidate sequence of domestic priorities, external pressure, incremental adjustment and continued domestic governance. No necessary loss of autonomy.

**Sonderfall** (GaE) — Special-case identity and associated political arguments; not a fixed motive of all Swiss actors.

**Sovereignty Erosion** (GaE) — Hypothesised deterioration on specified dimensions of autonomy or influence. Agreements may expand some options while restricting others.

**Variety Gap** (GaE) — Task-relative diagnostic concern; use separate axes in corrected VI/VIII. The former composite and critical threshold are withdrawn, not a national measurement.

**Vernehmlassung** (CH) — Consultation procedure; consultation and a legally binding veto are distinct.

**Veto Topology** (GaE) — Pattern of decision rules and blocking powers; distinguish legal veto, consultation and influence, and admissibility from timing.

**Vorbild** (GaE) — Exemplar; an openly advocated possible orientation towards cooperation, not a mandated endpoint.

**Zauberformel** (CH) — Magic formula, the informal Federal Council power-sharing arrangement described by the original report; verify historical and current details.
