## Appendix C: The Governance as Engineering Connection

### C.1 The Architectural Foundation

This report draws on Governance as Engineering's conditional formal analyses and analogies. The mathematical witnesses below concern specified models, not measured universities. The source invokes neither the I–V historical bridge nor the capstone retention bound. Accepted formal results constrain the argument; they do not establish an institutional diagnosis, policy mandate or universal law of stability.

### C.2 The Seven Primitives

The original uses seven structural primitives as analogies: **nodes** (departments, institutes, researchers, students and external partners); **state** (relevant knowledge, institutional arrangements and resources, not a presumed vector space); **flows** (evidence, methods, funding and communication, distinct processes); **latency** (time to discover, interpret, validate and use information); **constraints** (authority, resources, rights, integrity and academic freedom); **feedback loops** (actual evidence-to-decision and learning pathways); **signal fidelity** (validity of relevant representations and translations). Each requires an explicit task and a justified operational definition. A missing flow may be legitimate, and a person is not simply a node to be controlled.

### C.3 Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety

Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety is retained conditionally: in specified models, regulating to a task-defined acceptable set requires adequate distinguishable responses to relevant disturbances. The original maps the controller to university governance (incentives, evaluation mechanisms and organisational structures), and disturbances to the multidimensional challenges the institution may be asked to investigate. This analogy assigns no task or authority. W1 distinguishes information; W2 lawful admissible response, capacity and implementation, with the XX action-compatible cover bound necessary, not sufficient; W3 timing and quality of exchange. No universal stability or single-scale impossibility, constitutional unobservability or comparison of disciplinary dimensions follows. The historical formulation is a verification lead.

### C.4 The Variety Gap

Paper VI's original objective-as-observation inference is withdrawn. Evaluation objectives, incentives, access to evidence, attention, methods, knowledge validity and response authority are distinct. A university may observe both collaboration and quality while rewarding one; it may reward a proxy without observing the relevant outcome. The variety gap is a task-relative ambiguity or response question under specified premises (W1/W2), not an empirical dimension count. Paper VIII and XX do not establish an Integrative Capacity Score or universal proxy failure (W4). The capstone interaction witness is conditional (W8); the subsidiarity agreement-event distinction does not establish accuracy (W9).

### C.5 The Organizational Reports Series

The original places this report third in the Organizational Reports Series after AI and Healthcare, extending a country-report programme. Sequence, claims of historical results and provenance await author confirmation. Accepted corrections R4–R7 distinguish conditional strategic feasibility, institutional versus system-wide responsibility, protective constraints, personhood and net burden. They are not empirical evidence for Universities. The original's claim of 'standard mathematics' is limited to the assumptions and accepted results in the witnesses below.

### C.6 Conditional formal witnesses W1–W9

#### W1 — Task-relative observation
For linear disturbance subspace D, measurement C, acceptable ambiguity subspace L, K=D∩ker C, exact task-relative identification modulo L requires K⊆L. Hidden task-relevant ambiguity `h=dim K−dim(K∩L) ≥ max(0,dim D−dim(D∩L)−rank(C|D))`. In R³ with D=R³ and `L=span(e2,e3)`, observing x1 alone gives h=0; observing x2,x3 gives h=1 despite greater rank. No empirical dimension count follows; a department's knowledge is not a vector space by stipulation.

#### W2 — Objective, access, authority and action
A university can observe both research quality and collaboration while funding on the first only; it can reward collaboration while possessing only a noisy proxy. Objective ≠ observation. For deterministic action on observation-equivalence class E, intersection over x∈E of admissible acceptable action sets A(x) must be nonempty. If two states have the same observation but `A(0)={a}`, `A(1)={b}`, a≠b, there is no such action; if both contain a common action, distinction is unnecessary for this task. Separate lack of knowledge, lawful authority, capacity and execution. A research group may identify a policy option but have no legal authority to enact it; another owner may legitimately act. The XX action-compatible cover bound is necessary, not sufficient. In specified models requisite variety concerns task-relevant disturbances and distinguishable responses, not all of society. No universal stability or dimensionality comparison follows.

#### W3 — Network, synthesis and timing
Take expertise labels A,B,C with relevant tasks requiring only pair A,B. Graph G has edge AB; H has edges AC,BC but no AB. If direct AB exchange within a deadline is required and indirect paths cannot meet it, G meets the task despite fewer edges. If mediation via C is valid and timely, H may also suffice. Neither edge count nor co-authorship proves knowledge integration. For two estimates of an unknown scalar with unbiased independent errors of variance 1, the mean has variance 1/2; with identical errors it has variance 1. Under a stipulated shared bias of 2 and no noise, agreeing estimates both equal truth+2; agreement is not accuracy. Incompatible constructs or methods cannot be averaged without a justified mapping; preserve uncertainty and valid disagreement. For piecewise constant state with timely reliable notifications at each change, event-triggered monitoring can suffice; ten periods with flips at 3,7 and notifications two periods late yield stale periods 3,4,7,8. No universal single-scale impossibility or co-location necessity.

#### W4 — Proxy and composite ranking reversal
Hypothetical research-unit designs have (cross-disciplinary participation, independently assessed quality)=(0.8,0.4) and (0.6,0.9). Equal weights give 0.6 vs 0.75; weights (0.8,0.2) yield 0.72 vs 0.66. Weight choice is normative, and uncertainty and comparability matter. More joint appointments, grants, citations or coauthorships need not imply better scholarship. Independent integrity and rights limits cannot be compensated by a higher score. Keep the original six Audit questions separate; no validated composite, ranking or weight vector follows. XX deterministic proxy ordering does not ensure ordering under stochastic outcomes: on `X={(0,0),(0,1),(2,0),(2,1)}`, `M=a`, `T=a+b`, strict increases in M preserve deterministic T order, yet a lottery on (2,0) with probability 1/4 and (0,0) otherwise has `E[M]=E[T]=1/2` vs deterministic (0,1) with `M=0,T=1`. No universal proxy impossibility either.

#### W5 — Net burden and transfer
Stipulate 100 time units: 40 teaching/essential supervision, 20 discretionary administration, 40 research. Remove 10 administrative units but add 6 audit and 7 coordination units: 37 research units remain. A pilot releasing 10 units of participant teaching but assigning them to another worker does not remove institutional teaching obligations; total release is not 10 unless replacements and downstream workload are accounted for. These are accounting identities, not estimates of work or quality. Coordination may be valuable; test net contribution, opportunity costs and burden distribution, including graduate students, support staff and nonparticipants. Necessary ethics, accessibility and integrity work is not waste by definition.

#### W6 — Selected feedback and agency
In a stipulated frame of 100 eligible students, 20 respond, 12 report curricular incoherence. Respondent fraction 3/5; population fraction between 12/100 and 92/100 absent assumptions. If the frame omits students who withdrew or never enrolled, bounds do not cover those groups. Participation is endogenous to workload, trust, access, preference and legitimate refusal. No report does not establish absence of concern; dissent does not imply deficiency. Voluntary accessible feedback, privacy, no retaliation or individual profiling. Wellbeing, learning and preference are separate outcomes; bounds do not identify causes.

#### W7 — Bundled Pilot identification
Let Y(i,s) denote outcome under incentive/resource change i and shared-space/coordination change s. Tables ordered 00,10,01,11: (0,2,0,2) and (0,0,2,2) both yield bundle contrast 2, but incentive-only effects 2 and 0 respectively. Even randomizing the bundle does not identify components. Specify meaningful treatment versions (teaching relief, tenure recognition, seed support, graduate funding, co-location), unit/estimand, assignment, comparison, exchangeability, positivity, spillovers, common ascertainment, pretrends where relevant and uncertainty. Match alone not sufficient. Small selected site can establish feasibility, not general efficacy. Career, learning and integrity protections are not experimental concessions. No inference that a null or successful pilot proves H0–H5.

#### W8 — Interaction and conditional feedback
From a common baseline, two interventions each improve outcome by 1; joint improvement 1 or 4 gives interaction −1 or +2. Institutes, tenure changes, grants and infrastructure may substitute or complement. No proof that all are necessary, or that beneficial feedback must be self-reinforcing. A stipulated institution responding to a reporting complaint by removing duplicate reporting can break a burden loop. Preserve link-level hypotheses and independent reform evaluation; no guaranteed deterioration or survival inference.

#### W9 — Correlated review error
For two reviewers with hazard-conditioned miss probability p and correlation rho, joint miss `J=p²+rho p(1−p)`. At p=1/20, independent J=1/400; J≤1/100 iff rho≤3/19. Feasible rho∈[−1/19,1]. If either flags, miss J; if both must flag, miss 2p−J. Distinguish false alarms, validity, independence, downstream correction and authority. Shared training, data, disciplines or models can correlate errors; adding reviewers or AI cannot by itself ensure accuracy. Subsidiarity W5 agreement-event distinctions are not accuracy. W9 applies equally to discipline panels, integrative panels, research audits and this model-family-overlapping review. No empirical accuracy claim or need for central arbiter follows.
