# Part VI — Design principles for adaptive governance architectures

These nine principles are design proposals [IP], not deductions that guarantee learning or survival. Their common purpose is to keep consequential evidence and feasible correction paths available while respecting rights, safety and legitimate decision authority.

## 6.1 Protected experimental spaces

Local trials, bounded pilots and independently monitored comparisons can reveal responses that routine operation does not. Specify the decision the evidence will inform, the comparison and the stopping rule. The appropriate information requirement depends on the task, not a universal quota of variation. Natural variation or existing data may make an experiment unnecessary.

## 6.2 Safe-to-fail structures

Contain the downside of an experiment, identify affected parties and provide rollback, redress and monitoring. Reversibility of a rule is not reversibility of every consequence. Consent and applicable rights constrain the admissible experiment set; a possible information gain does not override them.

## 6.3 Separation of exploration and exploitation functions

An evaluator independent of the delivery body may detect errors in the latter's model or incentives. Separation is one arrangement, not a theorem that distinct organizations are always better. Assess shared dependencies, correlated blind spots, coordination costs and who can act on a finding. The corrected Paper XIX distinguishes actual warning or connective contribution from descriptive ranks.

## 6.4 Protected curiosity budgets

Reserve some resources for questions not selected by the current operational performance measure. An independent allocation procedure and transparent review can reduce short-term capture. The appropriate amount is an empirical and normative decision; no fixed exploration bonus is derived here.

## 6.5 Model review and replacement

Periodically test predictions, assumptions, excluded variables and rival explanations. Preserve evidence against the prevailing model and make a route to revision available. Do not compel a paradigm change merely because a review date arrives. A review must be able to conclude that the current model remains adequate for the specified task.

## 6.6 Institutionalized forgetting

Version policies and evidence, retain provenance and retire outdated operational assumptions without destroying the historical record. Forgetting in an estimator is downweighting, not erasure of archives. Sunset reviews may help remove obsolete rules, but their credibility depends on evidence and decision authority as well as formal expiry.

## 6.7 Learning rate accelerators

Shared data standards, replication, evaluation infrastructure and cross-jurisdictional transfer can shorten the time between evidence and revised decisions. Measure error, delay and reuse on specified tasks; faster updating can propagate error as readily as insight.

## 6.8 Learning from bounded stress exposure

Unexpected events and controlled stress tests can expose neglected response mechanisms. Useful information depends on the observation design and on whether tests transfer to the relevant environment. Learning from stress is not proof of antifragility; deliberately exposing people to harm is not justified by an abstract excitation condition. Use safe surrogates, natural evidence and protected testing when feasible.

## 6.9 Evidence-coupled sunset clauses

An effective intervention can make the event it prevents rare. In a stylized static model with threat rate \(\lambda\), unknown effectiveness \(e\) and probe exposure fraction \(p\), the operational record is

\[
Y_{\mathrm{reg}}\sim\operatorname{Poisson}((1-p)\lambda(1-e)).
\]

For positive operational exposure, the likelihood cannot separately identify the two factors. A known-coefficient probe \(Y_{\mathrm{probe}}\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(p\lambda(1-e_{\mathrm{probe}}))\) restores joint rank when both channels are informative (Appendix A.4). This is one mechanism by which successful regulation can complicate review, independent of the possibility of incumbent resistance. Nonidentifiability alone does not imply that a removal decision is impossible: a bound on the compatible threats may already suffice. Independent effectiveness evidence, comparable jurisdictions and other causal designs may also help. Comparison jurisdictions are not automatically comparable, decorrelated or costless.

Under a scalar local-Gaussian random-walk approximation, a probe design can be assessed against a steady-variance target. The approximate rate has fourth-power dependence on the confidence quantile and inverse log safety margin; Appendix A.4 gives the exact constant-information recursion and its conditions. Neither is a universal minimum experimentation rate or a theorem that removal never occurs below it.

The legacy sunset simulation reports [HR] no removal at \(p=0\) within 300 years at the tested drift levels, and median removal times around 70–80 years at \(p=0.15\) in scenario A. These reports have not been independently reproduced from raw outputs. The actual observer is a finite-grid filter with an informative prior, operating on mean-reverting truth while assuming a random walk. Scenario B counts any removal, not unsafe removal at the realized threat; probe incidents are total counts until removal, not incremental harm. The supplied legacy trade-off image says 30 years although the horizon is 300 years; the original images remain in the evidence archive, not in the revised argument. The reported known-effectiveness control is not implemented in the supplied script.

A review should specify acceptable risk, admissible evidence, uncertainty, the authority to renew or repeal, and safe reinstatement where feasible. Where direct experimentation is unsafe, evidence may have to come from models or indirect observations. Neither automatic repeal nor automatic renewal follows from the rank result. There is no derived exemption from review for any class of risk. The hypothesis that information deficits explain some pro-forma renewals is testable separately from capture, and has not been established by this model.
