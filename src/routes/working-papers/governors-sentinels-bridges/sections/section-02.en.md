## 2. Phenomenon, not identity

### 2.1 The methodological hazard

The pilot motivated P1–P4; it is not additional confirmatory evidence. The supplied preregistration specifies twenty zoos, seven fixed model types, the stream structure, thresholds and null clauses. It tests recurrence of a score pattern rather than the identity of a named model. Repetition across seeds does not itself validate a construct: the same estimator can reproduce an artifact in every ecology.

### 2.2 What the method commits to

Preserve the stated gates and distinguish a failed registered test from a favorable subcontrast. In this edition [HR] identifies historical reports without recoverable result tables, [R] exact results under stated assumptions, [E] exploratory checks, [H] hypotheses and [IP] institutional interpretations. The registration document is available; historical chronology is not independently authenticated. The supplied script differs from the registered design in timing, regime graph accounting and seed plumbing (Appendix A). Thus none of the historical counts is upgraded to a verified online or functional result. Future tests would require a common selection benchmark, pinned seeds before construction, tie-aware score summaries, zoo-level uncertainty and evaluation of roles on independent tasks.
