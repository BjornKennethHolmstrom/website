## 4. Governor and sentinel — nominal scores, not verified warnings

The governor score is negative mean stream loss. The nominal sentinel score counts covered spike episodes while the candidate is suppressed. P2's reported top mismatch is 17/20 and pooled Spearman correlation 0.481 [HR], numerically meeting the original thresholds. The positive correlation is not evidence of orthogonality. Under an illustrative independent uniform unique-top baseline, expected mismatches are $20(6/7)=17.14$ and the probability of at least 17 is about 0.68. This is not a calibrated test for the actual ecologies. Integer coverage scores permit ties; in synthetic checks the governor belongs to the maximum-coverage tie set in 61% but is picked by the first-maximum rule in 21%. Historical tie sets are unavailable. Active-model suppression couples the scores with no general sign.

The implemented centered 20-origin convolution uses origins $t-10$ through $t+9$, so its latest outcome is $t+28$. A nominal 10–50-step lead becomes −18 to +22 relative to spike issuance, −14 to +26 relative to the first target outcome, and +1 to +41 relative to complete revelation. This corrects only the centered-statistic clock; it does not establish a workable online detector. A synthetic witness in the correction archive produces a nominal hit using later losses. A true positive counts a covered episode, whereas a false positive counts an unsuccessful candidate timestep; only the earliest spike in the window receives credit. The resulting precision is not conventional alarm-level precision.

The historical P2 criterion is reported, but the causal warning and functional dissociation interpretations are withheld. A future test must match alarms to issuance-time failure events using information then available, measure incremental warning over an incumbent baseline, specify false-alarm units and lead requirements, and account for correlated errors and alarm costs. A poor governor may be a useful sentinel [H]; this experiment does not demonstrate that proposition in real time.
