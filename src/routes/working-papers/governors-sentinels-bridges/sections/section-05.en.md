## 5. Portfolio construction — a registered failure

P3 compares greedy marginal episode coverage at portfolio size $K=4$ with the four highest governor scores. The reported result is 11/20 wins and mean advantage of two covered episodes [HR]. The pass requires 12/20 and positive mean advantage; **P3 failed as registered.** A positive mean does not alter the gate. The same-sample greedy coverage procedure can benefit from selection on the evaluated episodes; and the underlying detector has the timing and accounting limitations of §4. No inference about held-out online warning is warranted.

Complementary coverage is a plausible portfolio objective [H], not necessarily the right objective when alerts are redundant, costly or unusable. Independent evaluation should compare marginal information after existing sensors, matched portfolio budgets, false alarms, and decision value before the deadline. No universal design rule follows from P3.
