## 8. What this paper does not show

The historical results do not establish online adaptive superiority, timely warning, independent latent role dimensions, functional translation, or a universal failure of winner-take-all selection. Loss histories need not identify predictions or internal representations: two opposite predictions can have identical squared losses on the same target. Graph centrality is relational and threshold-dependent. The experimental substrate is a small supervised prediction problem, not an institution acting on its environment. Preservation and switching costs, strategic actors, collective action, rights and legitimacy are not modeled.

A next experiment would be justified only for a specified claim: a causal warning policy, held-out portfolio value, or functional mediation under controlled ablations. The available source does not support estimating their effects. Retraining without the historical seed and cache provenance would be a new experiment, not reproduction. None is necessary to make the present correction.
