## 7. Historical calibration: reproducibility audit

The original Appendix B contains 20 rows (15 country/regional and five organisational cases), not 21. The former calibration used diagnoses already known when the proxies and scores were chosen. This is not independent validation. Neither the raw sources, elicited joint distributions, draws nor score-generating script were supplied. Appendix B of this revision records the formula discrepancy, not new scores.

An additional audit applied the original D.5 classification rule to the published rounded scores and Table 7.1 labels. Excluding Possible, counting Feasible (including qualified cases) as adequate and Difficult, Impossible and Generational as inadequate, 15 cases remain. With adequate defined by G below a cutoff, cutoffs from 3.21 to 3.50 on a 0.01 grid separate all 15; 2.0 separates 9. These are checks of the printed record, not a new estimated threshold. The old labels also reverse the direction in D.6 relative to §5.3; Spain's reported 2.6 is outside the stated ±0.5 band around 2.0. All threshold and trajectory classifications are withdrawn.

The printed latency median for the seven strictly Feasible cases is 18 months; including Japan and Spain gives 20, rather than the claimed 12. The printed Finland, Sweden and Germany oscillation values have median 0.08, not 0.20. The underlying time series and reference selection are not available. Reference constants must be documented choices until verified.
