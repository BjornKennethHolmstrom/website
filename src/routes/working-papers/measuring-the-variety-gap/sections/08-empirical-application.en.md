## 8. From pilot claims to a prospective protocol

The original pilot concerned Canada, South Korea and Argentina [HR]. Its claimed estimates, intervals and predictive agreement are not verified. The cases were chosen with knowledge of prior outcomes or capacity; a dated independent selection record, raw coding sheets, outcome records, and consent-safe evidence for interviews were not supplied. South Korea is now listed among the country reports; the claim that it was outside the original series needs a date and archived case list. No accusation about how the records were produced follows from missing provenance. The pilot-validation claim is withdrawn.

A future test could select a specific service pathway and freeze (1) task and outcome criteria; (2) case selection and a temporal cutoff; (3) observation and coding instructions; (4) candidate alternative explanations and predictions; (5) independent outcome assessment. Include adverse cases and nonresponses. Separate development cases from held-out cases; audit leakage when an outcome is also used as a predictor. Have independent coders record disagreement and unresolved evidence. Compare the profile against simpler baselines (e.g. latency only or existing service measures) and report false positives, false negatives and unknowns relative to the frozen task. This is a proposed protocol, not a completed study.
