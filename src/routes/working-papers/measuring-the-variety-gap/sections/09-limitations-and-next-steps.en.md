## 9. Limitations and next steps

This revision withdraws all historical composite point scores, reported uncertainty intervals, threshold bands, calibrated trajectories and claims of independent pilot validation as active results. It retains the measurement questions and source leads, not the old ranking. The task-relative hidden gap is identified only where the model and inputs permit it. A profile itself is not a tested measurement instrument. The old country narratives are not adjudicated here; they, their public score tables and the Variety Gap tool need separate reconciliation. Do not transfer a corrected description into those surfaces as evidence that the underlying data have been corrected.

Recovery of the original computation, any transforms, raw parameter estimates, distributions, code, seed, dated interview protocol and outcomes could support an archival reconstruction. It would not by itself cure the mismatch with VI. Next steps are a narrowly specified prospective case, independent coding, and a comparison to simpler alternatives before any aggregation. Normative task selection belongs to affected people and legitimate processes, not to the index. This is an invitation to test a method rather than accept a country diagnosis.
