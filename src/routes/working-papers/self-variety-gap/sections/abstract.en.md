## Abstract

This companion paper explores a conditional analogy between the task-relative adequacy of institutions and personal reflection. Values, observations, interpretations, decisions and feasible actions are distinct. A formal counterexample shows why counting values cannot establish self-observability; a replay of the legacy simulator exposes an asymmetric plant penalty and the limits of its pooled state metric. Voluntary reflection, relationships and revisability remain proposals; nondual and contemplative passages are philosophical, not clinical or neural deductions. The original universal self-variety gap, dissolution threshold and time-to-crisis prediction are withdrawn. No psychological scale, diagnosis or therapeutic efficacy is established. [M] exact scoped mathematics; [I] implementation; [HR] historical report; [E] exploratory model computation; [IP/H] interpretive proposal.
