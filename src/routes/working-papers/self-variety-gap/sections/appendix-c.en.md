## Appendix C: Research leads, not validation

| Topic in the original literature discussion | Question for future source review |
|---|---|
| Self-regulation and self-complexity | Which construct was measured, and is it comparable to the proposed task-relative distinctions? |
| Dissonance and motivated interpretation | How do evidence access, beliefs and goals interact under specific conditions? |
| Developmental stages and flow | Are outcomes operationalised independently of a proposed developmental hierarchy? |
| Mindfulness and acceptance | Which population, comparison, outcome, adverse effects and follow-up were studied? |
| Default-mode network and contemplative experience | What neural or experiential variable was measured, and what conclusions are not licensed? |

The original evidence table is withdrawn as support for a universal self-gap mechanism. These questions neither endorse nor reject the cited studies' own findings. Source-level review would be required before drawing substantive psychological or clinical conclusions.
