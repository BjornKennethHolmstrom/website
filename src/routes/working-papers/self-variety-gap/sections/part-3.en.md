## Part III: Domains of reflection

Truth, meaning, connection and health were the original paper's proposed missing dimensions. They remain possible domains of inquiry, not an exhaustive set of independent variables, a prescription for everyone, or signals with measured accuracy. A person may find other domains more salient or find that these overlap.

| Domain | Optional question |
|---|---|
| Truth | What evidence or perspective might change my interpretation? |
| Meaning | What feels worth doing or experiencing, without having to justify it as productive? |
| Connection | Which relationships or kinds of solitude matter to me now? |
| Health | What needs, limitations or uncertainties deserve attention or support? |

A response may involve seeking information, changing a plan, asking for practical help, resting, or doing nothing for now. One can value something without making it a target. Some experiences cannot be adequately described by a metric. The questions are invitations, not an assessment instrument or a demand to improve.
