# §7 — What this paper does not show

## 7.1 Geometry is not refuted in general

Three registered geometry failures are finite-panel findings, not equivalence tests. Neither regime-invariant rankings nor smooth topology are established. Test 3 shares dependent invariants by construction; the exact MST identity requires no empirical geometry assumption.

## 7.2 The predictive claim missed its threshold

H_predict fails on the original row unit. Source–regime aggregation is post hoc and changes the predictor. The measured association does not establish a universal ceiling for a symmetric predictor of directed cost.

## 7.3 The staging mechanism remains unresolved

H_geodesic and H_route pass their specified comparisons, with in-sample selection. H_hard and H_waypoint fail. The candidate set of the mechanism test admits destination warm-up; excluding it makes the mechanism test inconclusive. A longer or differently staged path can still be geodesic in another state space. No general optimal route is inferred.

## 7.4 Cost is relative to the adaptation process

The reference is capacity-matched but not certified converged; evaluation samples differ. The clipped budget proxy is not the cost of completing an institutional transition. Asymmetry sensitivity does not decompose causes. A stronger transfer claim needs matched held-out loss curves and a justified reference.

## 7.5 The object's calculus is open

Canonical single-leg costs are not actual sequential edge costs. A directed path geometry can be constructed if adequate states, edges and nonnegative costs are supplied. The paper does not estimate it.

## 7.6 One substrate and limited samples

Twenty geometry zoos, ten cost seeds and the smaller mechanism cube cover one family of prediction tasks. No training was reproduced by the correction. Matrix/table replay cannot establish robustness to other substrates, historical seed chronology or real institutions. No new experiments are required to withdraw unsupported claims.

## 7.7 Numerical and procedural correction

The full-panel statistics replace the first-three values. The original v1 cost outputs were overwritten or not recovered; the separate pairing analysis reports n/a, and the v4 summary supplies the paired figures. The original 25% source-dependence statistic and numerical triangle-violation rate lack recoverable support. The mechanism verdict is qualified by candidate-set sensitivity. All exploratory re-scores are explicitly labelled; they do not amend the registered gates.
