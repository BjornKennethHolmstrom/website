# §8 — Integration with the series

## 8.1 Against Paper XIX — a shared correction

XIX's P4 uses full-stream RMS loss dissimilarity, betweenness and articulation at the first connected threshold. It did not establish bridge function behaviorally by a separate mediation task. Its graph statistic can arise from scalar loss differences alone. XXIII uses squared prediction dissimilarity on other zoos. At the threshold, 13/20 of these zoos meet the regime-dependence criterion; at registered slack, 2/20 do. This is not a replication of XIX and neither paper demonstrates functional translation or bridge independence. The role of a bridge is an empirical design question.

## 8.2 Against Paper XXII — a rhyme, not a dependency

The gap between an observable proxy and a desired capability is a shared theme with XXII's certification questions. These experiments do not establish a corrupted certification kernel or the impossibility of assurance; loss and centrality are not certification outcomes. Use the distinction as a methodological reminder only.

## 8.3 Against Paper 0 and Paper XX — separate premises

Paper 0 motivates comparing bounded representations; corrected XX distinguishes action coverage, proxy–target ordering and certification assumptions. The present asymmetry is measured relative to a training procedure and reference, not derived from a representation bound. Even a highly expressive controller might have asymmetric update costs under some conditions, but no necessity follows. Corrected VI's distinction between model, observation, evaluation, decision and action is consonant with not treating behavioral dissimilarity as intervention cost.

## 8.4 Forward

A future study could predefine a causal warning or mediation task for XIX, or a held-out reference and route choice for XXIII. New training would be a new experiment. The two papers' joint correction preserves their questions and exact results without promising those extensions. The recovered archives and scripts allow scrutiny of the present evidence; downstream series summaries require separate reconciliation.
