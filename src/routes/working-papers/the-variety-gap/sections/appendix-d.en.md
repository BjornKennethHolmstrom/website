## Appendix D: Country Report Diagnostic Hypotheses

The previous table assigned heuristic objective counts, gap values and critical-threshold statuses to eleven systems. These numbers and statuses are withdrawn as current claims: they were not measured and the proposed common threshold is not established. They remain in the archived original edition. This replacement preserves the case coverage and diagnostic vocabulary, not the numerical rankings. These are hypotheses based on earlier reports, not updated country assessments.

| Country/system | Prior report emphasis | Candidate diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| Japan | Stability/continuity | Is renewal constrained despite available demographic signals? |
| Russia | Control/regime survival | Is independent evidence suppressed, unused or unavailable? |
| UK | Centralized delivery | Does local evidence reach decisions, and can local actors implement responses? |
| Brazil | Coalition survival | Are preferences observed but overridden in bargaining, or not transmitted? |
| EU | Member-state consensus | Do coordination delays arise from information, legal competence or vetoes? |
| USA | Federal integration | Do cross-state coordination and learning fail despite available information? |
| Germany | Execution | Is implementation limited by capacity, finance, information or strategic choice? |
| France | Integration | Do reform feedback and peripheral participation change implementation? |
| Sweden | Feedback/sensemaking | Are delays due to sensing, processing, incentives or response constraints? |
| Finland | Foresight | Can anticipated changes be acted upon within required time and resources? |
| India | Synchronisation | Where do cross-scale information, formal capacity and timing diverge? |

For each row identify a specific service or decision pathway, its goal and constraints, competing mechanisms, and independently measured outcomes. A crisis in the original report must not both define the predictor and validate it. No numerical gap, rank or threshold crossing follows from this table. A full country-source audit is outside this correction.
