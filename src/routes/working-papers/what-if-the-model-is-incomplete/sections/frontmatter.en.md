> **Evidence convention.** As in Working Paper 0:
>
> - **[R]** marks a result established within the specified computational model. Its status is stated alongside it:
>   - *confirmatory*: a prespecified test on the committed 40-world sample;
>   - *prespecified descriptive*;
>   - *exploratory*: declared in advance on development worlds, or post hoc;
>   - *structural*: an exact property of the model.
> - **[IP]** marks an interpretive proposition.
> - **[H]** marks a hypothesis for future investigation.
>
> The fine-grained status codes are given in Appendix A. A result inside a synthetic model is not, by itself, evidence about real catchments or institutions.
