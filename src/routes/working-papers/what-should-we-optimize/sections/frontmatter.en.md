> **Evidence convention.** **[R]** denotes a result established within one of the specified computational models (with confirmatory or exploratory status stated separately). **[IP]** denotes an interpretive proposition derived from comparison or conceptual analysis. **[H]** denotes a hypothesis proposed for future investigation. Neither a mathematical model result nor its confidence interval is, by itself, evidence about real institutions.
