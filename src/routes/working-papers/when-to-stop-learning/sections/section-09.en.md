## 9. Method and confidence

Tiers follow the series: **[R]** rigorous, **[IP]** in principle, **[H]** heuristic, with **[R within the model]** marking a result exact for the stated model and claimed no further. This paper is primarily conceptual, with a single registered simulation — the learning/adaptation demonstration of §3 — in `paper_xxi-learning_adaptation_preregistration.md` and `paper_xxi-learning_adaptation_demo.py`, thresholds and nulls fixed before the run, including the first operationalization's failure kept on record. The explorations behind the arguments (`10-learning-versus-adaptation.md`, `11a-evolving-update-rules.md`, `11b-sunsetting.md`) are the source of the framing and are cited as argument, not evidence.

Confidence by result:

| Result | Tier | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Learning/adaptation are distinct (§2) | [R] for the separation, [IP] for governance | Definitional; the demo makes it mechanical |
| Learning can break coupling by outrunning absorptive capacity (§3) | [R within the model] | 30 registered seeds; one environment; a revised operationalization |
| Adaptive/irrelevant/maladaptive taxonomy (§2.3) | [IP] | Sorts learning by effect on the loop |
| Bounded meta-learning hierarchy must close (§4) | [R] | Follows from finiteness of representation |
